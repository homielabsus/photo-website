No stiff posing, no over-editing. A relaxed few hours together, and a gallery that looks like you on a good day.

What clients say

Before you book

How do I book a shoot?
Start with a free 15-minute intro call. We talk through your idea, the vibe and the details, then lock a date. A small deposit holds the slot.
Do you travel outside the Bay Area?
Yes. Anywhere in the greater Bay Area is included; travel beyond that is quoted case by case on the intro call.
When do I get my photos?
A sneak-peek gallery lands within 48 hours and the full edited gallery in about ten days. Weddings run a little longer.
Film or digital?
Both. The look is film-first, but every shoot has a digital backup rolling so you never lose a frame that matters.
Can I use the photos for my business?
Brand and product shoots come licensed for web and social. Extended or print licensing is available on request.
